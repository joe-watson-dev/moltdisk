icon: open source licenses grant copyright permissions, not trademark rights.

People trust that an app called MoltDisk is this app, which matters for software that deletes files. So if you
publish a fork or a modified version:

- Use your own name and icon, not "MoltDisk" or the MoltDisk icon.
- Say that your project is based on MoltDisk, with a link to https://github.com/joe-watson-dev/moltdisk.
- Don't suggest that your project is endorsed by, affiliated with or the official version of MoltDisk.
- Don't use the MoltDisk name or icon to market a paid or competing product.

You may use the name to refer to the project accurately, for example in an article, a review or a list of tools,
or to say that your work is compatible with or based on MoltDisk.
